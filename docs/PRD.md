# Product Requirement Document (PRD) — PulseFit AI

**Project Name:** PulseFit AI  
**Project Description:** Autonomous AI Agent for Hybrid Fitness Analytics & Personalized Coaching.  
**Author:** Nachiket Gadilohar  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

### 1.1 Problem Statement
Fitness enthusiasts and athletes use multiple wearable devices and tracking apps (Apple Health, Strava, FitBit), but raw biometric data lacks actionable, personalized coaching intelligence. Generic fitness apps offer static workout templates that ignore fatigue, heart-rate variability (HRV), and recovery needs.

### 1.2 Solution: PulseFit AI
PulseFit AI is an autonomous AI fitness coach and biometric analytics engine. It ingests wearable telemetry, calculates daily recovery scores, predicts overtraining risks, and dynamically generates adaptive workout and nutrition plans via conversational AI agents.

---

## 2. Target Users & Personas

- **Athlete Alex**: Needs daily workout adjustments based on HRV and sleep telemetry.
- **Fitness Enthusiast Sam**: Wants conversational nutrition advice and automated progressive overload tracking.

---

## 3. Core Features
1. **Biometric Telemetry Ingestion**: Parse CSV/JSON logs from wearables (heart rate, HRV, sleep stages, active calories).
2. **Autonomous AI Coach Agent**: Conversational agent providing daily workout recommendations based on recovery scores.
3. **Adaptive Workout Engine**: Automatically modifies exercise sets/reps based on user feedback and fatigue metrics.
4. **Interactive Analytics Dashboard**: Visual charts for volume load, HRV recovery trends, and body composition progress.
